    Rules reference ▾

    Ladder (by points): Point 1 ▸ Line 2 ▸ Triangle 3 ▸ Square 4 ▸ Pentagon 5 ▸ Hexagon 6 ▸ Heptagon 7 ▸ Octagon 8 ▸ Nonagon 9. A card is worth its points (corners); the circle is 0.

    Direction — you set it. Play a form one rung higher than the centre and the turn goes right; one rung lower and it goes left — the very next number either way. You never play the centre form itself.

    Distance — you choose who's next. Play several copies of that shape and the turn travels that many seats — two cards skip one player, three skip two. No limit. That's how you decide who plays next.

    Circle — break the order. Legal on any form. Name the next shape, choose a direction, worth 0. Never stacks. The card everyone fears.

    Walls — no wrap. At the Point (bottom) you can only climb up; at the Nonagon (top) only down.

    Cornered — if you can't step, stack, or break the order, draw one card. Your turn ends (you don't play the card you drew).

    Win — first to empty their hand takes the round.
