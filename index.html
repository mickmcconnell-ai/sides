    Rules reference ▾

    Ladder (by points): Point 1 ▸ Line 2 ▸ Triangle 3 ▸ Square 4 ▸ Pentagon 5 ▸ Hexagon 6 ▸ Heptagon 7 ▸ Octagon 8 ▸ Nonagon 9. A card is worth its points (corners); the circle is 0.

    Play up or down. Each turn, play a form one or two rungs above the centre (up the ladder · clockwise) or one or two below (down · counter-clockwise). You never play the centre form itself.

    Stack for distance. Play several copies of that same up/down shape to move that many seats — two squares = two seats, three = three. No limit. Which way you go sets the direction.

    Circle — the only wild, legal on any form. Name the next form and choose a direction. Moves one seat. Never stacks.

    Walls — no wrap. At the Point (bottom) you can only go up; at the Nonagon (top) only down.

    Blocked — if you can't step, stack, or circle, draw one. Play it if it's legal, otherwise pass.

    Win — first to empty their hand, even on a stack or circle.
